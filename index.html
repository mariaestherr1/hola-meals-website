Meet Esther

Made with love in Utrecht, rooted in Monterrey.

I’m Esther Rodríguez, originally from Monterrey, Nuevo León, México. Food has always been close to my heart. In 2014, I moved to the Netherlands to complete a Master of Science in Food Technology at Wageningen University, and I have worked in the food industry as a professional food product developer ever since.

For five years I was happily vegan. Then I became a mother, and found myself craving the dishes I grew up with—the flavours of home that feel especially meaningful when home is far away. Cooking them for my own family brought that connection back.

It also made me understand, very personally, how difficult it can be to combine a full-time job, family life and putting something varied and delicious on the table every evening. Hola Meals! grew from that reality: thoughtfully developed food for busy households, a different menu every week, and always one authentic Mexican dish sharing a little piece of my home.

Sustainability matters deeply to me, but I also know that food choices have to work in real family and social life. My own experience taught me both the value of plant-based eating and the challenges of making it fit everyday life. Hola Meals! is not about one way of eating: it brings together meat, vegetarian and plant-based dishes, with care for nutrition, flavour, seasonality and a lighter footprint where possible. Above all, I want nutritious, sustainable and genuinely delicious food on my family’s table—and yours—rooted in the Mexican heritage I am proud of, especially the flavours of Monterrey, Nuevo León.

Who is responsible

Hola Meals! is responsible for processing personal information collected through this website and newsletter. For privacy questions or requests, email holamealsutrecht@gmail.com.

Information you send

The order form prepares an email in your own email application; the website itself does not submit or store that order form. When you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address and subscription record are sent directly to Brevo, our email marketing provider. Depending on your request, the information we process may also include your name, phone number, delivery address, order choices and delivery notes.

Why we use it

We use order information to answer your request, confirm availability and allergens, arrange delivery and payment, fulfil an order and maintain any legally required administration. With your consent, we use your email for news and menu updates. Newsletter subscriptions use double confirmation, and you can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every newsletter or by emailing us.

Retention and sharing

We keep correspondence only as long as reasonably needed for your request or customer relationship. Financial and order records are retained for the period required by Dutch law. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe, after which a limited suppression record may be retained to respect your choice. We do not sell your information. Brevo processes newsletter information on our behalf; we otherwise share information only with service providers needed to operate the service, or where required by law.

Cookies and website technology

This website does not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or visitor analytics. It saves only your Dutch or English language choice in your browser’s local storage. You can remove this through your browser settings. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages and loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. The newsletter form loads the technical components needed to submit subscriptions securely to Brevo. These providers may receive standard technical information such as your IP address. Google Search Console is used outside the website to monitor search visibility and does not place tracking cookies on this site.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict or object to its use, or withdraw newsletter consent. Where applicable, you may also request data portability. Contact us at the email address above. You may lodge a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
